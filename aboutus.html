Tasty Bites

About Us

Welcome to Tasty Bites, an authentic Indian restaurant that brings you the rich flavors of India, China, and the exquisite taste of Tandoori dishes. Established in 2023 by Nimisha Subhash, our journey started with a vision to serve food that feels like home while offering an unforgettable dining experience.

Our Specialties

  • Indian Cuisine: From aromatic biryanis to spicy curries, experience the true essence of India.
  • Chinese Delights: Enjoy flavorsome noodles, Manchurian, and delicious fried rice.
  • Tandoori Treats: Relish smoky, grilled dishes straight from the tandoor.

Our Mission

At Tasty Bites, our goal is to provide our customers with the highest quality food, made with fresh ingredients, and served with love. We strive to create a warm and welcoming atmosphere where families and friends can enjoy great food and create unforgettable memories.

Visit Us

We invite you to visit our restaurant in T. Nagar, Chennai, and indulge in a delightful culinary experience. Whether you're craving spicy Indian curries, sizzling Chinese stir-fries, or the smoky flavors of Tandoori, we have something special for every food lover.
